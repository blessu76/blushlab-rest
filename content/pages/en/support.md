## Frequently asked questions

### Is Blushlab out yet?

Not yet. Blushlab is in development for iOS and Android. Store badges on this site are placeholders until then.

### Is it free?

Blushlab is free to start, and in-app purchases are available. There are no ads.

### Do you collect personal information?

The Blushlab game does not collect personal information. This website collects basic visit statistics through Google Analytics (see the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) for details), and has no accounts or ads.
