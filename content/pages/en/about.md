Blushlab is a game that combines a makeup studio and a My Face mode in one app. It is made by **ipe()** (ipe Studio) and is **in development** for iOS and Android. There is no store page or release date yet.

## What it includes

- **Makeup Studio:** apply face-makeup tools (shadow, liner, lashes, blush, lip, brows and lenses) to a face, step by step.
- **My Face:** use a photo of your own face. Photos are processed on your device and are not sent to a server.

## Price and ads

Blushlab is free to start, and in-app purchases are available. There are no ads.
