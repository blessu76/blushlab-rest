Blushlab is being prepared in 12 languages: English, Korean, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French, German, Indonesian, Thai, Vietnamese and Turkish.

This site and its journal follow the same list. English and Korean are the original texts, and the other languages are translations of English.

## FAQ

### Which languages are included?

English, Korean, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French, German, Indonesian, Thai, Vietnamese and Turkish.

### Which texts are the originals?

English and Korean. The other ten languages are translations of the English text.

### Is Blushlab out yet?

Not yet. It is in development for iOS and Android, and there is no store page or release date.
