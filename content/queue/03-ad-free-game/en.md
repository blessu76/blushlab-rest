Blushlab has no ads. We want the time you spend with it to be about makeup, not about closing pop-ups.

The game is still in development, so we will share more as it takes shape.

## FAQ

### Will the game show ads?

No. Blushlab has no ads.

### Where can I follow progress?

Right here in the journal, where we post updates as the game takes shape.

### Is Blushlab out yet?

Not yet. It is in development for iOS and Android, and there is no store page or release date.
